# LinkedIn — use the News (and Gallery) templates

There is **no landscape LinkedIn template** in [folder FAHUw3DTDC0](https://www.canva.com/folder/FAHUw3DTDC0). LinkedIn native image posts display **4:5** well. Do not invent a 1200×627 graphic with `generate-design`.

| User asks for | Copy | Size |
|---------------|------|------|
| LinkedIn post / LinkedIn image / “Canva for LinkedIn” | **News** cover `[1]`, or cover + Key Facts `[1, 2]` | 1080×1350 |
| LinkedIn photo essay | **Gallery** | 1080×1350 |
| LinkedIn and they also want an IG Story | News **and** Stories as two separate copies | 1080×1350 and 1080×1920 |

Read `news.md` or `gallery.md` plus `images.md`. Same logos, lime, charcoal, paper, chips, and photo-accuracy rules as Instagram feed.

## Do not

- `generate-design` with `facebook_post`, `infographic`, or any other type “because LinkedIn is 1200×627.”
- `resize-design` a copied template to 1200×627 unless the user **explicitly** wants a landscape link-preview crop (that crop will break type; warn them).
- Mix GBA Pulse print red/gold into these social templates.

## Deliver

Same as every other format: export PNG, put the **image in the Slack / chat thread**, and include the Canva edit link. See SKILL.md → Deliver.
