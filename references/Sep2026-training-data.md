# Sep2026-training-data

Region and story-type targets for the Daily Brief. Fit on **35 colleague editions** (3 August–18 September 2026) in [The Bay 簡報 - 8月與9月](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1AzenY2Njzt0gik5edhjjl34mPjLo-Q5l).

Counted: the colleague’s own sections only. Pasted blocks headed “from The Pulse” are excluded. Automated files `Training Data/YYYY-MM-DD-daily-brief.md` are **not** part of this fit.

Machine-readable copy: `references/Sep2026-training-data.json`.

## What the colleague wrote

1,605 stories. About 46 a day (median 45, range 33–79).

| Band | Share | Per day |
|---|---|---|
| Hong Kong | 22% | 10 |
| Shenzhen | 20% | 9 |
| Macao | 19% | 9 |
| Nation | 11% | 5 |
| Around the world | 10% | 4–5 |
| GBA News | 8% | 4 |
| Guangzhou | 6% | 2–3 |
| Zhuhai inc. Hengqin | 4% | 2 |

Type mix: **85% hard news, 12% lifestyle, 3% award**.

Lifestyle is a dated cultural or leisure event (festival, exhibition, concert, museum show). Award is a result (medal, champion, final). Business rankings such as WIPO stay hard news.

Foshan, Dongguan, Huizhou, Zhongshan, Jiangmen and Zhaoqing had no section quota. A story about one of those cities goes in the section of dominant impact, usually GBA News.

## Targets for a 40-story edition

Collect **100** fresh candidates before ranking. Publish at most **40**.

| Band | Target |
|---|---|
| Hong Kong | 9 |
| Shenzhen | 8 |
| Macao | 8 |
| Nation | 4 |
| Around the World | 4 |
| GBA News | 3 |
| Guangzhou | 2 |
| Zhuhai inc. Hengqin | 2 |

Type targets: **34 hard news, 5 lifestyle, 1 award**.

A band may finish one story light when that band has no fresh approved item. Name the gap in the audit. Do not pad.

June editor marks (`[selected]`, society, GBA integration, weather) break ties **inside** a band. They do not change these counts.
