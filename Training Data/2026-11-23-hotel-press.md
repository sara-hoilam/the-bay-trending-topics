Sands China (3):

1. **Sands China’s Community Revitalization Programme Unveils ‘The Hall of Glory – Iec Long Firecracker Factory’** — Sands China Press
https://assets.sandsresortsmacao.cn/press-release/2026/scl/1117-the-hall-of-glory-opening_en.pdf
Since joining the Macao SAR government’s historic-district revitalisation effort in 2023, Sands China has unveiled The Hall of Glory – Iec Long Firecracker Factory, a new permanent attraction on the original site. Developed with digital heritage experts Professor Jeffrey Shaw and Professor Sarah Kenderdine plus local academics, the interactive exhibition focuses on passing down Macao’s century-old firecracker culture.

https://assets.sandsresortsmacao.cn/press-release/2026/scl/1110-macau-band-30ann-concert_en.pdf
