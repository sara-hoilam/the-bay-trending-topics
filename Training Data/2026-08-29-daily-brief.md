Macao (8):

4. **Macao pledges RMB30 million for Gyirong disaster relief** — Macao SAR Government
https://www.gov.mo/zh-hant/news/1261936/
The Macao SAR will donate RMB30 million via the Macao Foundation to the Xizang Autonomous Region after the Gyirong Port mudslide. Chief Executive Sam Hou Fai had already conveyed condolences to Party secretary Wang Junzheng and said Macao would also offer medical and other support if needed.

5. **July e-payments rise 3.1 percent at restaurants, 6.3 percent in retail** — DSEC
https://www.gov.mo/en/news/407346/
DSEC said electronic payments at restaurants reached MOP1.16 billion in July, up 3.1 percent year on year, while retail e-payments rose 6.3 percent to MOP4.26 billion, led by cosmetics and jewellery. Year-to-date restaurant turnover is up 1.9 percent to MOP8.08 billion and retail 14.3 percent to MOP33.71 billion, covering about 70–80 percent of receipts.

6. **Hotel occupancy 91.6 percent in July as guest numbers fall** — DSEC
https://www.gov.mo/en/news/407348/
Average hotel occupancy rose 0.5 percentage points year on year to 91.6 percent in July, with five-star houses at 94.2 percent, but guest arrivals fell 4.3 percent to 1.229 million, including a 13.8 percent drop from Hong Kong. International guests rose 9.5 percent to 89,000; inbound package-tour visitors fell 6.7 percent to 141,000.

7. **Resident unemployment holds at 2.4 percent in May–July** — DSEC
https://www.gov.mo/en/news/407350/
DSEC said the local-resident unemployment rate stayed at 2.4 percent in May–July, with underemployment down 0.1 point to 2.2 percent; 7,100 residents were unemployed, mainly from gaming and retail. The overall jobless rate was 1.9 percent; the labour force, including about 112,000 cross-boundary workers, rose 700 to 495,800.

8. **New school year: 70,000 tertiary students and AI teaching tools** — DSEDJ
https://www.gov.mo/zh-hant/news/1261833/
DSEDJ said 10 higher-education institutions enrol about 70,000 students this year, while 75 non-tertiary schools (118 campuses, including Hengqin) serve about 80,000 pupils, 105 of them in free education. University Town Phase 1 is running 39 postgraduate programmes; a smart-teaching platform and AI materials go live, and an Education Fund “AI experimental classroom” grant is added.

9. **Macao and Shenzhen drug regulators widen medical-device cooperation** — Macao SAR Government
https://www.gov.mo/zh-hant/news/1261912/
ISAF director Choi Peng Cheong met Shenzhen market-regulation chief Liu Guangyang on expanding information sharing, joint anti-counterfeit checks and multi-centre trials after Macao’s medical-device law took effect on 1 July. About one-quarter of Macao-approved new drugs are from the mainland, including 24 first launched after mainland listing; the Shenzhen institute will extend testing support from medicines to devices and advanced therapies.

10. **CCAC visits Foshan, Zhongshan and Hengqin integrity bases** — Macao SAR Government
https://www.gov.mo/en/news/407397/
Commissioner Against Corruption Ao Ieong Seong led a CCAC and disciplinary-committee delegation to integrity-education bases in Foshan and Zhongshan and met Hengqin discipline officials on industry and youth-employment risks. The group toured University Town Phase 1 and the China–Portuguese/Spanish-speaking countries service centre; Ao said CCAC wants a joint integrity safeguard for the cooperation zone.

11. **Transport and security hiring up in second quarter** — DSEC
https://www.gov.mo/en/news/407355/
DSEC said employment in transport, storage and communications rose 11.4 percent year on year to 16,436 at end-June, while security jobs grew 2.9 percent to 13,590; wholesale and retail fell 1.6 percent to 65,803 and gaming 0.3 percent to 52,992. June average earnings were MOP14,860 in wholesale and retail and MOP28,660 in gaming; security’s recruitment rate was 6.3 percent.
