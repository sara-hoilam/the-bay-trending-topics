https://www.gcs.gov.mo/news/detail/zh-hant/N26IJx1DaE
Macao’s Health Bureau, the Hengqin cooperation-zone executive committee and Gongbei Customs signed a joint-supervision pact yesterday for blood, urine and stool samples from the Macao New Neighbourhood clinic. Gongbei will inspect every consignment; the “Macao person, Macao sample, Macao test” service is due in the first quarter of 2027 once supporting facilities are ready.

3. **Shenzhen raises RMB 1 billion in Macao via first GBA sustainability bond**
https://www.gcs.gov.mo/news/detail/en/N26IJx1ivD
https://www.gcs.gov.mo/news/detail/zh-hant/N26IJx1ivD
Shenzhen’s government sold a three-year RMB 1 billion offshore note in Macao yesterday at 1.41 percent, its second Macao issue and the first Greater Bay Area-themed sustainability bond there. Proceeds are earmarked for water-governance and healthcare projects; AMCM said the HKQAA-certified deal was oversubscribed.

4. **Macao and CMA start Hengqin earth-system data hub**
https://macaubusiness.com/macau-joins-hengqin-in-building-earth-data-system/
Construction began yesterday on a China Meteorological Administration data-exchange hub in Hengqin, the first under the agency’s overall plan, with Macao’s meteorological bureau as a partner. CMA director Chen Zhenlin said the three-year build plus three years of operations will support Macao’s diversification and export China’s early-warning tools.
