## How sources are combined in practice

- **Macao story:** TDM + `gov.mo` (+ Macao Business / modaily for English)
- **HK story:** TVB + SCMP or Standard + `info.gov.hk`
- **GBA mainland:** NewsGD/SouthCN + city daily (`gzdaily`, `sztqb`)
- **Cross-border:** TVB + SouthCN + Xinhua gangao section
- **Nation:** Xinhua English / CGTN + TVB SC + Reuters
