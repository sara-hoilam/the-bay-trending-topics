6. **Cash-sharing plan optimisations** — gov.mo + TDM
https://www.gov.mo/zh-hant/news/1241457/
https://www.tdm.com.mo/zh-hant/news-detail/1208337
The Social Welfare Fund and IAM held a press conference on 2 June to introduce optimisations to the 2026 cash-sharing plan for Macao residents who were outside the SAR for part of 2025. Under the scheme, permanent residents receive 10,000 patacas and non-permanent residents 6,000 patacas if they spent at least 183 days in Macao during 2025; residents claiming one of eight approved absence categories may now submit declarations for up to three years, with an improved Macao One app workflow. Officials said about 10,000 eligible residents outside Macao will receive payouts automatically this year without filing again, including through a trial with Hengqin community offices to pre-issue residence proof for residents living in the cooperation zone; residents working in the GBA accounted for roughly 30 percent of the more than 56,000 exemption declarations received for the 2025 cycle.

11. **April monetary and financial statistics** — gov.mo
https://www.gov.mo/zh-hant/news/1241617/
AMCM figures released on 3 June show Macao’s broad money supply M2 fell 0.6 percent month-on-month in April to 854.4 billion patacas, with M1 down 8.1 percent as current deposits dropped 11.4 percent while currency in circulation edged up 0.1 percent.
