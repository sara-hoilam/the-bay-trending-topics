Macao (6):

https://macaupostdaily.com/news/29463

https://macaupostdaily.com/news/29462
SSM said 11 boys and seven girls at Pui Va Middle School’s primary section have shown rash and blisters since 15 September; all had one varicella dose and none was severely ill. Staff disinfected the campus and kept sick pupils at home, calling the outbreak a breakthrough-infection cluster.

Cheong said the anti-fraud centre flagged a middle-aged man and woman on 16–17 September and froze their accounts after they ignored calls. The woman was stopped from sending HK$1.5 million; the man had not yet transferred funds.

9. **Sam says Chi Lam Vai renewal plan is ready** — Macau Post Daily
https://macaupostdaily.com/news/29455
The chief executive told Monday’s urban-renewal leadership group that a detailed Chi Lam Vai revitalisation plan is finished, with Wong Sio Chak and Raymond Tam reporting from the law and works groups. The third five-year plan gives a dedicated chapter to speeding protective renewal around the Ruins of St Paul’s.

10. **PJ deepens GBA and Interpol police cooperation** — Macao SAR Government

The Judiciary Police said it is tightening intelligence links with the mainland, Hong Kong and Interpol to protect Hengqin and the GBA, including FRONTIER+ anti-scam work with more than 10 places. Two officers now hold Interpol affiliate posts; the bureau cited recent serious drug-trafficking cases blocked as transit.

11. **UTM–UNWTO course opens on AI destination branding** — Macao SAR Government
https://www.gov.mo/zh-hant/news/1268273/
The 18th Macao University of Tourism–UNWTO training course opened yesterday at MGM, themed “reshaping destination brands with AI and digital marketing”, with a fourth year of MGM sponsorship. The four-day programme brings Asia-Pacific tourism officials together with UNWTO, MGTO, Sun Yat-sen University, Fliggy, Klook and Tencent.

https://news.rthk.hk/rthk/ch/component/k2/1871196-20260923.htm

A turnaround paper is due to the chief executive by the first half of 2027; probation staff will get two-year, not permanent, contracts.
