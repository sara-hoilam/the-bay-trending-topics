GBA News (5):

1. **Huanggang runs third joint vehicle-lane stress test**
https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/343663/Third-cross-boundary-stress-test-at-new-Huanggang-port-involves-over-1000-vehicle-trips
Hong Kong and Shenzhen teams yesterday put the new Huanggang “joint one-stop” vehicle lane through a third immigration stress test, with more than 1,000 closed-loop private-car and tour-bus trips. Customs selected about 40 vehicles for inspection and practised lane closures; Immigration said preparations for opening continue.

2. **Guangdong hosts GBA medical-products emergency drill**
https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202609/23/P2026092300287.htm
Hong Kong’s Department of Health joined yesterday’s Guangzhou drill run by the Guangdong Provincial Medical Products Administration with Macao’s Pharmaceutical Administration Bureau, simulating a Level II incident involving Hong Kong-registered drugs in the GBA. More than 300 people took part; DH said a Hong Kong Centre for Medical Products Regulation is due this year.

3. **ImmD sees 1.29 million mainland visitors in Golden Week**
https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202609/23/P2026092300605.htm
https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/hong-kong-economy/article/3368547/hong-kong-welcome-129-million-mainland-chinese-visitors-national-day-break
Chan Kwok-ki chaired yesterday’s festival working group as Immigration estimated about 1.29 million mainland arrivals from 1 to 7 October, with daily averages more than 5% above last year. The trade expects about 1,180 inbound tour groups; a police–ImmD–Customs joint command centre will run through the week.

4. **Hengqin “Meet Tech” reopens for Macao secondary students**
https://www.gov.mo/zh-hant/news/1268433/
Hengqin’s talent bureau and DSEDJ sent the 2026/27 first batch of 73 Macao secondary students through Hengqin Port yesterday to planning halls and AI, IT, new-materials and chip firms. The programme has taken about 500 students in 10 batches; the zone said it has hosted more than 33,800 Macao residents across some 450 visits.

5. **Sam and SPC’s Sun discuss Hengqin legal safeguards**
https://www.gov.mo/zh-hant/news/1268464/
Sam Hou Fai met Supreme People’s Court Political Department director Sun Zhenping at Government Headquarters yesterday on mainland–Macao judicial ties and legal support for Hengqin. Sam asked for continued SPC help on legal-system modernisation under the third five-year plan; Song Man Lei attended.


Macao (8):

6. **LegCo rejects EU’s 2025 Macao annual report** — Macao SAR Government
https://www.gov.mo/zh-hant/news/1268506/
The Legislative Assembly today “strongly disapproved of and firmly opposed” the European Union’s 2025 Macao report, including comments on the eighth LegCo election. It said candidate-eligibility rulings applied the electoral law and “patriots administering Macao”, and told Brussels to stop interfering in China’s internal affairs.

7. **Airport to close runway overnight for 2.5 years** — Macau Post Daily
https://www.macaupostdaily.com/news/29472
CAM said flights will stop daily from midnight to 08:00 from late October until the second half of 2029 for reclamation, new taxiways and runway works. The 129-hectare expansion is due in 2030, taking the airport to 325 hectares; CAM may shorten the nightly closure in peak holidays if safety allows.

8. **Timber forum closes with 140 pairings and 2 billion in deals** — Macao SAR Government
https://www.gov.mo/zh-hant/news/1268485/
IPIM said last night’s close of GLSTF 2026 produced more than 140 business pairings, six cooperation outcomes and contracts totalling 2 billion patacas. MUST joined the Global Timber Index research team, and Macao groups launched a green-supply-chain centre plus biomass-aviation-fuel standards work.

9. **PJ arrests three in 118 million-pataca buying-agent scam** — Macau Post Daily
https://www.macaupostdaily.com/news/29479
Cheong Kim Fong said 11 victims, including 10 residents, reported losses from 136,000 to 92 million patacas between May and September, with the largest order 20,000–30,000 jars of cordyceps. Suspects Tan, Iek and Ip are held; about 50 million patacas were spent personally and some funds moved to mainland accounts.

10. **Young woman lured to Southeast Asia on a “free trip”** — Macau Post Daily
https://www.macaupostdaily.com/news/29480
Cheong said a local woman met a man on Douyin this month, left via Guangzhou airport on 12 September and was held after arriving, with a gang member threatening to send her to a scam centre. Her father lost contact the next day and reported it on the 13th; PJ warned against free overseas trips and high-pay job offers.

11. **SSM logs three influenza-like school clusters** — Macao SAR Government
https://www.gov.mo/zh-hant/news/1268424/
The Health Bureau yesterday reported 22 patients since 18 September at Tung Sin Tong kindergarten, Yuehwa Form 2 and Pui Ching’s Coloane K3 class; 12 rapid tests were influenza A and none was severely ill. Campuses stepped up disinfection and isolation.

12. **SMG: Mid-Autumn night should stay clear for moon and fireworks** — Macau Post Daily
https://www.macaupostdaily.com/news/29478
SMG said high pressure should keep skies relatively clear tomorrow night, with good visibility for moon-watching and the 34th International Fireworks Display Contest. Days stay sunny and hot through the weekend; a tropical cyclone east of the Philippines may affect the Taiwan area next week.

13. **Wu Zetian and Luoyang show opens at the Macao Museum** — Macao SAR Government
https://www.gov.mo/zh-hant/news/1268470/
IC and Henan’s cultural relics bureau opened “The Empress’s Realm” yesterday at Mount Fortress, with 126 sets from eight Henan institutions, including 21 Grade-I pieces such as Wu Zetian’s gold-slip. The show runs to 28 February 2027; the museum stays open to 22:30 on 25 September and to 21:00 on 26–27 September, with free entry in those evening hours.


Hong Kong (9):

14. **August Composite CPI holds at 1.7%, underlying 1.9%** — Hong Kong Government
https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202609/23/P2026092300316.htm
C&SD said overall prices rose 1.7% year on year in August, with underlying inflation 1.9% after relief measures, both unchanged from July. Electricity, gas and water were up 11.5% and basic food down 0.2%; the first eight months also rose 1.7%.

17. **Sandy Ridge data hub aims to start in mid-2027** — SCMP
https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/hong-kong-economy/article/3368520/northern-metropolis-data-hub-start-phased-operations-mid-2027-clp-power

18. **Youth arrests rise about 20% in the first half** — SCMP
https://www.scmp.com/news/hong-kong/law-and-crime/article/3368475/juvenile-arrests-20-first-half-2026-driven-assault-damage-cases
Commissioner Joe Chow said 1,580 people aged 10 to 20 were arrested from January to June, about 20% more than a year earlier. Serious-assault cases rose 47% to 283 and criminal damage more than 50% to 140, including 48 debt-collection damage cases against 18 last year.
