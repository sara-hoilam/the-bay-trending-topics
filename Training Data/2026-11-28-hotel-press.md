3. **Sands China’s Community Revitalization Programme Unveils ‘The Hall of Glory – Iec Long Firecracker Factory’** — Sands China Press
https://assets.sandsresortsmacao.cn/press-release/2026/scl/1117-the-hall-of-glory-opening_en.pdf
Sands China unveiled The Hall of Glory at the former Iec Long Firecracker Factory, a permanent community-tourism attraction developed with new-media artists Professors Jeffrey Shaw and Sarah Kenderdine. The project preserves Macao’s century-old firecracker heritage on its original site through research, conservation and immersive technology.
