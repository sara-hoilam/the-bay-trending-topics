https://info.newsgd.com/node_a42013034a/acfce2f3c9.shtml
https://www.newsgd.com/node_d36b0ef83f/b89344d1fe.shtml
June 2026 marks three years since the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership entered into force. Customs statistics cited by Southern Net show imports worth 53.8 billion yuan through Guangdong ports have qualified for RCEP preferential tariffs since June 2023, with cumulative duty savings of 1.4 billion yuan. https://www.gov.mo/zh-hant/news/1241824/

As of 2 June, the Guangdong–Macao In-Depth Cooperation Zone counted more than 8,000 Macao-funded enterprises in operation, up 72.6 percent from the zone’s 2021 launch and representing 15.9 percent of all registered firms — roughly one in six businesses.
