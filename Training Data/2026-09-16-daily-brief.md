GBA News (3):

1. **Second-phase GBA civil-servant research exchange opens in Macao**
https://www.gcs.gov.mo/news/detail/zh-hant/N26IOz9SfA
Guangdong’s GBA office, the provincial Hong Kong and Macao affairs office and Macao’s civil-service bureau opened the second 2026 research-exchange at the University of Macau yesterday. GBA-city civil servants will hear Macao officials on governance and “One Country, Two Systems”, and visit national key laboratories in Chinese medicine, chips and lunar science.

2. **Hui: 80 redomiciliation applications, 50 approved; 3,380 family offices**
https://news.rthk.hk/rthk/ch/component/k2/1870048-20260915.htm
Financial Services Secretary Christopher Hui told the 12th Cross-Strait, Hong Kong and Macao economic forum that Hong Kong has received about 80 company-redomiciliation applications and approved 50, so overseas firms can use the city to reach both shores and the two SARs. Single family offices numbered more than 3,380 at end-2025, up about 680 in two years; gold central clearing is in trial with plans for physical delivery and Mainland gold-market links.

3. **“Million talents to South Guangdong” autumn drive opens in Beijing today**
https://news.southcn.com/node_556cf9f3e2/bbeb25cfa3.shtml
The provincial government, the Ministry of Education and Foshan open the 2026 N-city autumn campus tour today at Peking University, with about 1,000 Guangdong employer visits and more than 15,000 posts over two days at 11 Beijing and Hebei universities. Firms include Tencent, Midea, BYD, Gree and Peking University Shenzhen Hospital; Foshan said more than 260,000 young people have taken jobs there since 2025.


Macao (9):

4. **Health Bureau logs four new flu-like school clusters, seven A-positive** — Macao SAR Government
https://www.gcs.gov.mo/news/detail/zh-hant/N26IOHyNtw
Authorities yesterday reported four influenza-like clusters at Lou Hau affiliated primary, Kao Yip kindergarten, Kwong Tai (branch) and New Chinese School, covering 24 pupils. Symptoms began from 12 September; seven rapid tests were influenza A-positive, and all cases were mild.

5. **Employment coordination group’s sixth meeting focuses on youth jobs** — Macao SAR Government
https://www.gcs.gov.mo/news/detail/zh-hant/N26IOJQ9EC
Secretary for Economy and Finance Ng Wai Han chaired the year’s sixth meeting of the employment coordination group, with DSAL reporting nearly 50 youth job talks and more than 10 Mainland internships in the first eight months. From August the bureau has run hotel and catering matching fairs; it will take career talks into secondary schools and add outreach with youth associations.

6. **East Zone-2 B1 superstructure Zone 1 draws 24 bids** — Macao SAR Government
https://www.gcs.gov.mo/news/detail/zh-hant/N26IOhJ0h0
DSOP opened 24 tenders yesterday for Zone 1 superstructure on the 30,371-square-metre B1 education plot, accepting 22; bids ran from just over MOP460 million to more than MOP530 million for 503–504 working days. The package covers one school, the whole-plot basement and linking underpasses, with work due in the second quarter of 2027.

7. **August forex reserves rise 1.1 percent to MOP245.8 billion** — Macao SAR Government
https://www.gcs.gov.mo/news/detail/en/N26INOf2fz
https://www.gcs.gov.mo/news/detail/zh-hant/N26INOf2fz
AMCM said preliminary foreign-exchange reserves were MOP245.8 billion (USD30.44 billion) at end-August, up 1.1 percent from a revised MOP243.1 billion, equal to 11 times currency in circulation or 87.8 percent of pataca M2. The trade-weighted pataca index fell 1.00 point month-on-month and 1.37 points year-on-year to 99.5.

8. **July new home mortgages fall 40.9 percent; commercial loans jump** — Macao SAR Government
https://www.gcs.gov.mo/news/detail/en/N26INmmM3b
https://www.gcs.gov.mo/news/detail/zh-hant/N26INmmM3b
AMCM said new residential mortgages approved in July dropped 40.9 percent month-on-month to MOP862 million, while new commercial-property loans rose 191.6 percent to MOP3.03 billion. Outstanding home loans were MOP201.12 billion (down 4.8 percent year-on-year) with a 3.6 percent delinquency ratio; commercial loans were MOP132.32 billion, delinquency 5.6 percent.

9. **Macao–National Museum cultural-creative contest opens for entries today** — Macao SAR Government
https://www.gcs.gov.mo/news/detail/zh-hant/N26IOOuCWQ
The Cultural Affairs Bureau and the National Museum of China launched a Macao SAR–Ministry of Culture and Tourism contest at the Macao Forum yesterday, with the “Ancient China in Cultural Creativity” show. Applications run 16 September–30 October for residents aged 18-plus (up to five original works); gold, silver and bronze plus seven merit prizes will be judged on theme, market potential and design.

https://www.gcs.gov.mo/news/detail/zh-hant/N26IOyU9Mt

11. **DSSCU batches illegal corridor gates in 500-unit estate; 10 percent still non-compliant** — Macao SAR Government
https://www.gcs.gov.mo/news/detail/zh-hant/N26IO8WkQh
