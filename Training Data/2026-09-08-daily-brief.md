GBA News (6):

1. **Hengqin five-year tally: 105 million crossings, RMB190 billion trade**
https://www.newsgd.com/node_99363c4f3b/494afa28e2.shtml
NewsGD said five years after the Hengqin master plan, the Cooperation Zone’s trade reached RMB190 billion (up more than 20 percent) and Hengqin Port logged 105 million passenger trips and 12.45 million vehicle crossings. January–July trade hit a period record of RMB43.46 billion, up 64 percent; a road-air cargo service from the Hengqin pre-terminal has cleared more than 55 truckloads since 26 August.

2. **Hengqin and Macao open “R&D plus registration” path for new TCM**
https://www.gcs.gov.mo/news/detail/zh-hant/N26IGVSRCd
Macao’s drug regulator and Hengqin’s commercial-services bureau signed a memorandum yesterday so innovative Chinese medicines developed in the zone can enter a Macao registration channel after Hengqin’s first-pass check. ISAF will give pre-filing guidance and full-lifecycle tracking for jointly designated clinically urgent products, reversing last year’s “Macao drugs into Hengqin” flow.

3. **770 Hong Kong youths interned in seven GBA mainland cities**
https://news.rthk.hk/rthk/ch/component/k2/1869144-20260907.htm
Home Affairs and Youth under-secretary Leung Kong-yui said this year’s GBA internship sent more than 770 Hong Kong young people to seven mainland cities for chip, AI, digital-industry and public-service placements. A new Youth Self-recommendation round is open until 5 October for 16-to-35-year-olds seeking district youth-committee seats.

4. **Chan: GBA’s edge is finance plus innovation; summit moves to Dongguan**
https://news.rthk.hk/rthk/en/component/k2/1869086-20260907.htm
https://news.rthk.hk/rthk/ch/component/k2/1869092-20260907.htm

About 300 investors and more than 500 startups opened at Science Park yesterday before the event shifts to Dongguan and Hangzhou.

5. **Shenzhen ports pass 200 million crossings, 28 days earlier than 2025**
https://news.southcn.com/node_ac2b0b62a4/24b35e7d76.shtml
Southern Daily said Shenzhen’s ports had cleared 200 million travellers by 5 September, 28 days ahead of last year, with daily traffic at 80,860 (up 10.89 percent) and 29 days above one million. Hong Kong residents still made 132.5 million of the first eight months’ trips, while other overseas visitors rose 23.97 percent to 3.05 million.

6. **Guangdong highs 32–34°C through 9 September; two to three typhoons due**
https://info.newsgd.com/node_ce515f4756/2e9ceef4f6.shtml
NewsGD said White Dew usually cools the province, but 7–9 September will be cloudy to sunny with highs of 32–34°C (30–32°C in the east) and lingering humidity. The National Meteorological Center expects two to three typhoons to affect China this month and one or two South China Sea or northwest-Pacific disturbances in the next 10 days.
