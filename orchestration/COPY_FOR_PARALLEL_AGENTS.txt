REQUIRED STEPS (in order):

1. Open and capture each of these live boards RIGHT NOW (not from training data):
   - Google Trends Trending Now for HK, US, GB, MO, JP, SG, IN
     URL pattern: https://trends.google.com/trending?geo=HK&sort=search-volume&hours=48

   - Score each: volume (35%) + velocity (35%) + cross-platform (30%) = compositeScore
   - Sort descending by compositeScore
   - Include at least 15 candidates

4. Set windowHours: 48, refreshedAt to current ISO timestamp (HKT).

5. Keep disclaimer: "" (empty string — do not paste methodology notes into the banner).

6. Do NOT add the standalone preview ribbon.

7. Save to orchestration/fragments/trendwatch.html — keep the model-tag line, <svg> sprite
   (tw-icon-* symbols), and <div id="trend-watch-root"></div> unchanged.

You are writing the CLAUDE panel of GBA Pulse Top 10. Read the instructions below
carefully — previous runs produced stale content because this order was not followed.

⛔ DO NOT search for news yet. DO NOT open any news site yet.

MANDATORY WORKFLOW — follow this exact order:

STEP A1 — Parse the Trend Watch JSON from orchestration/fragments/trendwatch.html.
  Extract topicCandidates[] if present; otherwise build candidates from sections[] using
  the scoring spec in prompts/gba-pulse-trend-scoring.md.

  ⚠ COLLECT ALL GEOS: google_trends.itemsByLocation contains rows for HK, US, GB, MO,
  JP, SG, IN and possibly others. You must collect rows from EVERY geo — not just HK.
  A story trending in US, SG, or JP may be highly GBA-relevant and score high on
  cross-platform if it also hits Baidu or X. Skipping non-HK geos causes the Top 10
  to under-represent international trends that GBA audiences are tracking.

STEP A2 — Produce a self-audit table (write it as an HTML comment at the top of the
  fragment, e.g. <!-- TREND AUDIT: ... -->):

  Rank | Topic              | Score | Platforms       | Board evidence
  1    | [title]            | 84    | Google HK, Baidu | HK #1 20K+; Baidu #3
  ...up to 10...

  Any topic with empty Board evidence column = remove it, use next candidate.

STEP A3 — ONLY AFTER the table is written and validated: search for fresh news
  (prefer published within 48h of today) explaining WHY each of those 10 topics is
  spiking right now. News = context. News does NOT choose the topics.

STEP A4 — Write the HTML. For every topic:
  - .topic-meta 📊 line: "Trend score: NN/100 · N platforms (Platform1, Platform2)"
  - .why-box: write 2–3 bullets as OPEN QUESTIONS / EDITORIAL ANGLES for editors, e.g.:
    <div class="why-box">
      <strong>Interesting Angles</strong>
      <ul>
        <li>Where are the top bars to watch this match in Hong Kong, and are venues
            already seeing booking spikes?</li>
        <li>How are sports-betting markets pricing the outcome as the season closes?</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    These are QUESTIONS, not answers. Do NOT put trend-score platform chips inside
    .why-box — those belong in .topic-meta only.
  - Posted: use the actual article date. If article is older than a week, use
    "Posted: date not shown (accessed YYYY-MM-DD)" and explain the spike in the summary.

Output ONLY inner HTML. Same CSS classes as templates/gba-pulse-template.html.
No <html>, <head>, outer <main>. Write to orchestration/fragments/claude.html.

You are the Trending News merger for GBA Pulse (panel-overall).

MERGER RULES (in priority order):

1. ANCHOR ON TREND WATCH. Open trendwatch.html and re-check the topicCandidates[]
   scores. The merged Top 10 must be drawn from topics on those boards — if a
   model panel includes a topic with no Trend Watch board hit, exclude it from
   the merged edition (mention it briefly in Key Observations instead).

2. Among Trend Watch-anchored topics, rank by:
   - Composite trend score (highest first)
   - Cross-model overlap (both Claude and Composer > single panel only)
   - GBA/HK/Macao editorial relevance
   - Freshness of verification (prefer ≤48h articles)

3. Normalize duplicates from the **Claude and Composer panels only** into single entries.

4. If a panel included a topic with an article from weeks or months ago but
   that topic IS on the Trend Watch boards, keep the topic but update the summary
   to explain the CURRENT spike rather than the original story.

5. Do NOT promote any topic not on the Trend Watch boards, even if both panels
   mentioned it. Move it to Key Observations instead.

6. Do NOT use topics, rankings, or copy that appear only in chatgpt.html or any GPT run.

7. Content mix reference (tie-breaker only, not a quota):
   innovation/business ~50%, arts/culture ~30%, food/drink ~15%, other ~15%;
   mainland GBA ~50%, HK ~30%, Macao ~20%.

8. Every .topic-meta: source/date after location; 📊 line shows
   "Trend score: NN/100 · N platforms (…)".

9. Every .why-box: <strong>Interesting Angles</strong> + 2–3 bullet points written as
   open QUESTIONS or editorial prompts for editors — not explanations of why it's
   trending. E.g. "Where are the top watch-party venues in HK for this match?" Do NOT
   put platform chips inside .why-box; those belong in .topic-meta only.

Output ONLY inner HTML: ten .topic blocks, Key Observations, Sources, Notes for
Tomorrow. Do NOT include a <p class="agg-method">…</p> paragraph — it is hidden by
CSS and no longer shown to readers. No <html>, <head>, <body>, outer <main>.

Write to orchestration/fragments/overall.html.
