HZMB "Open" Car Permits: Application Window Closes Tomorrow — 1,400 New Quotas, No Macao Ties Required

Applications for 1,400 new three-year HZMB driving permits close on May 20 — the most significant expansion of the cross-boundary car scheme since the bridge opened in 2018, including 400 "open" category slots requiring no employment or business links to Macao, open to any Hong Kong permanent resident.

Why It's Trending
  • Deadline urgency (closes tomorrow May 20) is driving a Xiaohongshu "自駕澳門攻略" (self-drive Macao guide) surge and Reddit r/HongKong threads packed with application tips since early May.
  • SCMP, RTHK, and Macao News all ran features; RTHK reported the open-category eliminates the long-standing employment requirement for the first time — characterised as "no strings attached" by VisaHQ.
  • Successful applicants can drive into Macao's city centre from July 13, 2026 — triggering Macao tourism planning discussions on TikTok and Douyin (#澳門自駕遊).

Why It's Trending
  • IMF statement (May 15) led SCMP business section and circulated widely on X/Twitter HK finance accounts; Google Trends HK showed "Hong Kong GDP 2026" spike post-release.
  • IMF recommendation to introduce a goods and services tax triggered strong Xiaohongshu and Reddit r/HongKong public debate — GST has historically been politically toxic in HK.
  • HK$5.8B tax concession bill passed LegCo on May 13 (100% profits tax reduction capped at HK$3,000, ~171,000 businesses benefiting) — counter-narrative to IMF's fiscal consolidation advice.
