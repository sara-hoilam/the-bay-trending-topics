Guangdong dragon-boat calendar

At a press conference on 2 June, Guangdong officials said the province will hold 208 dragon-boat themed activities and 547 races between June and July, involving nearly 100,000 participants across all 21 prefecture-level cities. A provincial main venue event is scheduled for mid-June in Foshan, while Guangzhou will host the 41st International Dragon Boat Invitational on the Pearl River on 20 June with more than 100 teams expected. Officials also announced culture and tourism consumption vouchers, a 30-million-yuan “ticket economy” fund, and summer study-tour routes covering the province’s coastline and manufacturing hubs.

HZMB underwater tunnel vehicle fire

Traffic on the Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macao Bridge was disrupted on the afternoon of 2 June after a car caught fire inside the underwater tunnel on the section heading towards Zhuhai and Macao. The Transport Department said at about 1:42 pm that the tube towards Zhuhai and Macao would be fully closed; the fast lane reopened first, with the middle and slow lanes following before the route was fully restored at around 2:50 pm. Video circulated online showed smoke inside the tunnel and queues backing up onto the bridge; the cause of the fire was still under investigation.

Shenzhen robot exports

According to Shenzhen customs data cited by Southern Net, the city exported 4.03 billion yuan worth of separately itemised robots in the first four months of 2026, accounting for 25.5 percent of China’s national total over the same period. Southern Net said roughly one in every four yuan of China’s robot exports now comes from Shenzhen-made products, which were shipped to more than 100 countries and regions. The report highlighted firms such as Dobot, which has ranked first in China for collaborative robot exports for eight consecutive years and has surpassed 100,000 units shipped globally.

Macao

LRT–Hengqin linkage

Secretary for transport and public works Raymond Tam told legislators on 2 June that the government has begun preparatory work to connect Macao’s LRT network with the Hengqin extension line at two points: the existing Jockey Club station and, longer term, Barra station. Tam said Jockey Club currently offers better conditions to divert passenger flows and will interchange with the future Taipa Central Line, while Barra is being positioned as a hub where the Taipa Line will meet the planned South and West lines. He added that authorities are pushing to introduce QR-code fare payment across the LRT network in the third quarter of this year and are actively advancing the Southern LRT Line as the East Line is slated to open in 2029.

Macao

Project mBridge — day-one transactions

The Monetary Authority of Macao said on 3 June that three of the 11 banks cleared to use Project mBridge completed 23 cross-border transactions on the platform’s first day of operation on 2 June, with total value equivalent to nearly 1.3 billion patacas. Bank of China Macau, ICBC Macau and Bank of Communications Macau processed settlements involving mainland China, Hong Kong and the UAE, covering trade settlement and cross-border remittances; AMCM said the remaining eight participating banks are preparing to go live. Executive director Henrietta Lau said the launch marks a new stage in Macao’s integration into international digital finance and complements expanded sandbox testing of the digital pataca for e-government, public transport and campus use.

Macao

Cash-sharing plan optimisations

The Social Welfare Fund and IAM held a press conference on 2 June to introduce optimisations to the 2026 cash-sharing plan for Macao residents who were outside the SAR for part of 2025. Under the scheme, permanent residents receive 10,000 patacas and non-permanent residents 6,000 patacas if they spent at least 183 days in Macao during 2025; residents claiming one of eight approved absence categories may now submit declarations for up to three years, with an improved Macao One app workflow. Officials said about 10,000 eligible residents outside Macao will receive payouts automatically this year without filing again, including through a trial with Hengqin community offices to pre-issue residence proof for residents living in the cooperation zone; residents working in the GBA accounted for roughly 30 percent of the more than 56,000 exemption declarations received for the 2025 cycle.

Macao

Hotel occupancy and room supply

Lo Chi Fai, head of the Office of the Secretary for Economy and Finance, told legislators on 2 June that Macao had 147 operating hotels and about 45,400 guest rooms as of April 2026. Statistics and Census Service figures cited by the government show average hotel occupancy reached 91.8 percent for January–April, up 2.2 percentage points year-on-year, while April alone stood at 90.2 percent. Macao Business reported that 4.87 million hotel guests were recorded in the first four months, a record for the period, and that 11 new hotel applications submitted to MGTO in the first four months of the year would add about 2,000 rooms if approved.

SME digital support programme

Macao

Taxi licence tender — 700 vehicles

Macao’s Transport Bureau published a gazette notice on 3 June inviting bids for 14 taxi licences, each allowing up to 50 vehicles and carrying a reserve price of 2.5 million patacas for an eight-year permit. Bidders must be Macao-based companies with registered capital of at least 5 million patacas and have until 9 July to submit tenders. Macao Business said about 1,400 taxis currently operate in the territory and the new licences could raise the fleet to roughly 2,100 vehicles, against a backdrop of renewed ride-hailing interest and planned regulatory changes Secretary Raymond Tam flagged to legislators in January.

Macao

April monetary and financial statistics

AMCM figures released on 3 June show Macao’s broad money supply M2 fell 0.6 percent month-on-month in April to 854.4 billion patacas, with M1 down 8.1 percent as current deposits dropped 11.4 percent while currency in circulation edged up 0.1 percent. By currency, pataca, Hong Kong dollar, renminbi and US dollar accounted for 32.1 percent, 44.1 percent, 7.4 percent and 14.7 percent of M2 respectively. The overall loan-to-deposit ratio rose to 75.4 percent from 71.2 percent at end-March, while the non-performing loan ratio eased to 4.4 percent from 4.7 percent.

Hong Kong

Lee Kazakhstan visit — 43 MoUs

Chief Executive John Lee said in Astana on 2 June that Hong Kong had provisionally reached 43 memoranda and cooperation agreements with Kazakhstan, covering finance, healthcare, agriculture, media, aviation and green finance. Lee told reporters the SAR would negotiate to extend visa-free access for Hong Kong passport holders from 14 to 30 days and pitched the city as a “super connector” for Kazakh firms entering mainland and ASEAN markets. The visit — Lee’s second Central Asia trip and the largest trade delegation of his current term — included meetings with the prime minister, sovereign wealth fund Samruk-Kazyna and Astana Hub; media MOUs signed on the trip included a content partnership between The Standard and Freedom Horizons, and Lee was due to visit Nazarbayev University on 3 June before continuing to Uzbekistan.

Hong Kong

Retail sales up 8.6% in April

Provisional Census and Statistics Department figures released on 2 June showed Hong Kong retail sales totalled HK$31.4 billion in April, up 8.6 percent year-on-year — the 12th consecutive monthly increase. Online sales accounted for 9.7 percent of the total and rose 30.6 percent year-on-year to HK$3.0 billion; among physical categories, motor vehicles and parts jumped 46.1 percent and electrical goods 21.9 percent, while department stores fell 6.7 percent. A government spokesman said the sector should continue to benefit from economic expansion and higher inbound tourism, but SCMP cited Retail Management Association chair Sally Shung cautioning that a broad recovery has not fully emerged given outbound travel to the mainland and softer tourist spending.

Zhuhai inc. Hengqin

Youth talent subsidies

Hengqin’s youth talent subsidy scheme opened for applications on 1 June and will run until 30 June for degree holders, professionals and Macao youths working for employers registered in the Guangdong–Macao In-Depth Cooperation Zone. Eligible Macao youths can receive living subsidies of up to 270,000 yuan, while degree-holding and professional talents may receive up to 180,000 yuan and skilled workers up to 60,000 yuan, paid in instalments over two to three years depending on category. Applicants must have paid individual income tax and social insurance in Hengqin for at least six consecutive months if applying for the first time.

Guangzhou

Marine economy 2030 target

Guangzhou Daily reported on 3 June that a draft 15th Five-Year Plan for marine economic development, released for public consultation on 30 May, targets raising the city’s gross ocean product to 730 billion yuan by 2030. The document said Guangzhou’s marine economy reached 523.1 billion yuan in 2025, accounting for 16.3 percent of local GDP, and sets goals to build an internationally competitive marine industry cluster and strengthen the Southern Marine Science and Technology Innovation Centre. By 2035, the plan envisages Guangzhou as a modern maritime city with ocean-related industries ranking among global leaders.

Merck GBA innovation centre

German science and technology group Merck officially opened its Greater Bay Area Innovation and Collaboration Centre in Nanshan on 1 June, marking a major expansion of its China footprint after 93 years in the market. Qianhai international payment measures

Qianhai authorities jointly published ten measures to streamline international payment services in the cooperation zone, requiring key merchants and districts to accept foreign bank cards, cash and mobile wallets and to install ATMs and currency-exchange facilities within 500 metres of major tourist sites. Shenzhen Special Zone Daily said the package supports UnionPay promotions, palm and facial payment pilots at venues such as Qianhai Ice and Snow World, the “Bay Area Light” Ferris wheel and K11, and expands “external card binding” and “external wallet internal use” services — including 36 overseas e-wallets such as PayPal. The policy also references broader use of the mBridge platform to connect cross-border payment scenarios between the mainland and Hong Kong.

China–UK strategic dialogue

Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi and UK Foreign Secretary Yvette Cooper held the 11th China–UK Strategic Dialogue in Beijing on 2 June, pledging to deepen cooperation and stabilise ties after Prime Minister Keir Starmer’s visit to China earlier this year.
