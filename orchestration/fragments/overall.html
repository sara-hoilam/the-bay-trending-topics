Guangdong water sector reports zero casualties and no reservoir failures after Butterfly

Guangdong's water resources department said on 15 June that coordinated reservoir operations, patrols and pre-positioned teams achieved "no casualties, no reservoir failures, no major levee breaches and no critical infrastructure hits" during Typhoon Butterfly and the associated downpours.
