GBA hosts national Environment Day event in Guangzhou

Guangdong, Hong Kong and Macao are jointly hosting the 2026 National Event on the Environment Day in Guangzhou on 5 June under the theme “Comprehensive Green Transformation, Jointly Build a Beautiful China”, the first time the national flagship has been co-organised by multiple local governments. The 1–7 June Beautiful China Promotion Week pairs with the main ceremony, which is set to release national ecological achievements and hand over the 2027 host flag; Macao authorities said the SAR will run parallel education activities including facility tours, carbon-point rewards and beach clean-ups. The Ministry of Ecology and Environment preview said the programme will also launch a 2026–2030 national civic action plan and hold an inaugural Beautiful China lecture hall on the afternoon of 5 June.

RCEP third anniversary — Guangdong import tariff savings hit 1.4 billion yuan

June 2026 marks three years since the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership entered into force. Customs statistics cited by Southern Net show imports worth 53.8 billion yuan through Guangdong ports have qualified for RCEP preferential tariffs since June 2023, with cumulative duty savings of 1.4 billion yuan. Annual growth in savings was 8.81 percent in 2023, 32.35 percent in 2024 and 32.12 percent in 2025; Guangdong Customs said it will improve clearance services and cultivate authorised exporters to expand eligible beneficiaries across the GBA manufacturing base.

Construction site smoking ban — Labour Department clarifies “all reasonable steps”

A Labour Department paper to the Legislative Council on 4 June said contractors should install CCTV, set temporary cigarette storage points and stop e-cigarette use to meet “all reasonable steps” required once a site-wide smoking ban takes effect on 17 July, following the November 2024 Wang Fuk Court blaze that killed 168 people. Macao-funded firms in Hengqin exceed 8,000

As of 2 June the Guangdong–Macao In-Depth Cooperation Zone counted more than 8,000 Macao-funded enterprises in operation, up 72.6 percent from the zone’s 2021 launch and representing 15.9 percent of all registered firms.
