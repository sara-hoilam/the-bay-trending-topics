GBA News

Lee arrives in Uzbekistan; HK–Uzbekistan 30-day visa-free notes exchanged

Chief Executive John Lee arrived in Tashkent on the evening of 3 June for the second leg of his Central Asia tour after concluding the Kazakhstan leg, where his delegation provisionally signed 43 cooperation memoranda. On 3 June (Tashkent time) the HKSAR Government and Uzbekistan exchanged notes agreeing to negotiate a mutual visa-free arrangement allowing 30-day stays, extending the current 10-day visa-free access for Hong Kong passport holders visiting Uzbekistan and enabling all Uzbek passport holders to visit Hong Kong visa-free for 30 days. Lee said Uzbekistan’s young population, resources and market reforms create scope for finance, trade and Belt and Road collaboration, while RTHK reported Trade Development Council chairman Frederick Ma expects reciprocal business meetings to build on a recent Uzbek ministerial visit to Hong Kong.

Guangzhou–Astana direct flight launches

Air Astana flight KC8217 landed at Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport at 04:19 on 3 June, inaugurating the capital’s first direct link to Astana after the carrier began Guangzhou–Almaty service last year. Southern Net said the route operates twice weekly on Wednesdays and Fridays with Airbus A321LR aircraft, with KC8217 departing Astana at 18:45 (Tue/Thu) and arriving Guangzhou at 04:30 the next day, and return flight KC8218 leaving Guangzhou at 05:45. Baiyun airport has added Central Asian destinations including Almaty, Tashkent and Astana over the past year; official data cited more than 90,000 travellers from five Central Asian countries through Baiyun in January–May 2026, up 286.6 percent year-on-year.

RCEP third anniversary — Guangdong import tariff savings hit 1.4 billion yuan

June 2026 marks three years since the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership entered into force. Customs statistics cited by Southern Net show imports worth 53.8 billion yuan through Guangdong ports have qualified for RCEP preferential tariffs since June 2023, with cumulative duty savings of 1.4 billion yuan. Annual growth in savings was 8.81 percent in 2023, 32.35 percent in 2024 and 32.12 percent in 2025; Guangdong Customs said it will improve clearance services and cultivate authorised exporters to expand eligible beneficiaries across the GBA manufacturing base.

17th International Infrastructure Investment Forum (IIICF)

The 17th International Infrastructure Investment and Construction Forum and Exhibition will run from 10 to 12 June at the Cotai Expo, jointly organised by IPIM and the China International Contractors Association, with more than 3,500 political and business leaders confirmed. Macao officials said the forum has hosted 60 minister-level guests from Portuguese-speaking countries since relocating to Macao in 2012, with partnership agreements totalling USD 19.5 billion signed, and will release the 2026 Portuguese-speaking Countries Infrastructure Development Index report under the theme “Boosting Green Digital Infrastructure Connectivity”. Flagship projects cited include Brazil’s cross-sea bridge, Angola’s industrial park, Mozambique housing and transport facilities, and Timor-Leste drinking-water infrastructure.

Macao firms in Hengqin exceed 8,000

As of 2 June, the Guangdong–Macao In-Depth Cooperation Zone counted more than 8,000 Macao-funded enterprises in operation, up 72.6 percent from the zone’s 2021 launch and representing 15.9 percent of all registered firms — roughly one in six businesses. The government said leasing and business services (26.3 percent), wholesale and retail (21 percent) and scientific research and technology services (19.9 percent) lead the portfolio, with “four new” industries and modern services together accounting for about 37.5 percent of Macao investment. Officials highlighted benchmark firms including RISC-V chip designer Guangdong Yuefang Technology, which completed a B-round financing exceeding 200 million yuan in 2025, and Aobao Pharmaceutical’s “Macao registration + Hengqin production” model for classic Macao medicines.

Hong Kong

Wang Fuk Court administrator denied meeting extension

The Lands Tribunal on 3 June rejected Hop On Management’s application to extend the statutory deadline for convening an owners’ meeting at fire-hit Wang Fuk Court in Tai Po after receiving a petition signed by more than 5 percent of owners. Judge Gary Lam said the Buildings Management Ordinance gives the tribunal no power to extend the 45-day meeting window and that Hop On had already collected contact details for 1,601 of 1,984 units. Hop On, appointed after the November 2024 blaze, said it would continue verifying signatures; the Home Affairs Department said it would study the judgment.
