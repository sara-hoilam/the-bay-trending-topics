Heatwave breaks into thunderstorms across the Pearl River Delta

Hong Kong recorded its hottest day of 2026 so far on Friday as urban temperatures reached 34.6°C, with Yuen Long Park hitting 34.4°C by late afternoon — the highest on Grain in Ear since records began.
