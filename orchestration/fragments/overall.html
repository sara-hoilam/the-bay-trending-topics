Thunderstorms sweep Pearl River Delta after record heat

Hong Kong issued amber, yellow and red rainstorm warnings on Saturday as a broad trough brought thundery showers across the Pearl River estuary, following Friday’s hottest day of 2026 when Observatory headquarters recorded 34.6°C. Wan Chai temple and Stanley Mosque proposed as statutory monuments

The Antiquities and Monuments Office recommended on Thursday that Hung Shing Temple on Queen’s Road East in Wan Chai and the Stanley Mosque at Stanley Prison be declared statutory monuments after consent from owners and management bodies. Hung Shing Temple, believed built between 1847 and 1852, is among Wan Chai’s oldest temples, while the mosque is one of only two pre-war Islamic buildings surviving in Hong Kong. If the Antiquities Advisory Board and chief executive approve, gazettal would grant permanent statutory protection under the Antiquities and Monuments Ordinance.
